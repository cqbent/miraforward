## **MIRA Forward New England Endorses Governor Maura Healey, Lieutenant Governor Kim Driscoll, and Attorney General Andrea J. Campbell**

**BOSTON, MA –**  Today, MIRA Forward New England announces their endorsement for Governor Maura Healey, Lieutenant Governor Kim Driscoll, and Attorney General Andrea J. Campbell. 

“MIRA Forward New England was created to help defend the needs of immigrants across Massachusetts by supporting elected leaders, advocates, and supporters who come together and fight for immigrant and refugee rights,” **said the Board of Directors of MIRA Forward New England.** “We are incredibly excited to endorse Governor Maura Healey, Lieutenant Governor Kim Driscoll, and Attorney General Andrea J. Campbell in their re-election campaigns and have them continue to show their support in helping us advocate for our immigrant communities in Massachusetts.” 

The Massachusetts Immigrant and Refugee Advocacy (MIRA) Coalition launched MIRA Forward New England, a nonprofit political advocacy organization designed to recruit, support and elect leaders who will further immigrant and refugee support at a time when they are under direct threat around the globe, in March 2026.

MIRA Forward New England is proud to issue their first round of endorsements for the 2026 Midterm Elections. 

“Immigrants are an essential part of the fabric of Massachusetts – strengthening our economy, building businesses, caring for our loved ones and contributing to our communities,” said **Governor Maura Healey.** “As Governor, my focus has been on ensuring our immigrant families have the resources and support they need to succeed, while also protecting their rights and safety in the face of escalating attacks from President Trump and ICE. I’m proud to stand alongside MIRA in this important work and grateful for their support of our campaign.”

"As a proud first-generation American, I know how important it is for every family to feel welcome, safe and supported in their community. MIRA plays a critical role in making that possible in Massachusetts,” said **Lieutenant Governor Kim Driscoll.** “Public safety depends on trust, not fear, which is why our administration refuses to stand by while ICE tears families apart and makes us all less safe. Maura and I remain fully committed to partnering with MIRA to protect our immigrant neighbors and keep our communities safe.”

“Every person in Massachusetts, regardless of where they were born or what documents they carry, deserves to be treated with dignity,” said **Attorney General Andrea Joy Campbell**. “I am honored to earn MIRA Forward New England’s endorsement. When the Trump administration unlawfully targets our immigrant neighbors, we go to court. When employers steal wages from immigrant workers, we hold them accountable. When our most vulnerable need someone to acknowledge and protect them, I along with my team show up. That is the Attorney General I have been, and will continue to be.”
