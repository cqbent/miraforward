## **MIRA Forward New England Endorses Senate President Karen Spilka and House Speaker Ron Mariano** 

**BOSTON, MA –**  Today, MIRA Forward New England announces their endorsement for Massachusetts Senate President Karen Spilka and Massachusetts House Speaker Ron Mariano. 

“MIRA Forward New England was created to help defend the needs of immigrants across Massachusetts by supporting elected leaders, advocates, and supporters who come together and fight for immigrant and refugee rights,” **said Maroni Minter, MIRA Forward New England.** “We are incredibly excited to endorse Senate President Karen Spilka and House Speaker Ron Mariano in their re-election campaign and have them continue to show their support in helping us advocate for our immigrant communities in Massachusetts.” 

The Massachusetts Immigrant and Refugee Advocacy (MIRA) Coalition launched MIRA Forward New England, a nonprofit political advocacy organization designed to recruit, support and elect leaders who will further immigrant and refugee support at a time when they are under direct threat around the globe, in March 2026.

MIRA Forward New England is proud to issue their third round of endorsements for the 2026 Midterm Elections. 

“I am incredibly honored to have the endorsement of MIRA Forward New England which reflects my commitment to standing up for the rights of immigrant families and refugee communities in Massachusetts,” said **Senate President Karen Spilka (D-Ashland)**. “This session, we took nation-leading legislative action to protect our immigrant neighbors from ICE and the Trump Administration's inhumane and destructive policies. I remain steadfast in my commitment to protecting the rights of our immigrant neighbors and ensuring that Massachusetts is a welcoming place for everyone."

“Immigrants are essential to the fabric of Massachusetts, strengthening our neighborhoods, enriching our communities, and helping drive our economy. Despite incivility and threats on the national level, we will do what we can to ensure our immigrant neighbors can live their lives and raise their families with dignity and security,” said **House Speaker Ronald J. Mariano (D-Quincy).** “I’m proud of the House’s work, including the PROTECT Act, the Work and Family Mobility Act, and the Immigrant Legal Defense Fund. MIRA was a valuable partner on both bills, bringing the voices and experiences of immigrant communities to the table and helping us translate their needs into meaningful legislative action. I’m grateful for MIRA Forward New England's endorsement as we continue to work to ensure that Massachusetts remains a place where everyone has the opportunity to contribute and thrive.”
